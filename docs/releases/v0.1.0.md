**Pocket: preview release.** The whole Kompendium epistemické anihilace and the Prismatic
Platform's grading scales, compiled into a knowledge pack on the SD card and read by a native
FAP, offline: read it, recognise what is happening, act on it, grade your sources, and train.

### What is in it

- **Library**: 2 241 objects in 66 chapters and 11 kinds, by chapter, by kind, in pack order or
  at random; contents outline, map of links, confusables side by side.
- **Detect**: 1 212 detectors. You mark each marker observed, not observed, unknown or not
  applicable and see your marks back with what to rule out and what to do next; marks are saved,
  as JSON Lines, only when you ask.
- **Act**: 1 548 objects with steps, 1 128 confusable pairs, 5 assessments (Admiralty code, DD
  evidence levels, OSINT confidence), 2 scorecards and the Bayesian fusion calculator, all live:
  every key press recomputes the result.
- **Train**: 1 671 "which term?" questions, 1 185 "manipulative or benign?", spaced repetition in
  Leitner boxes kept on the SD card, and 9 crisis simulations.
- **Explorer**: the same pack in the browser at <https://korczis.github.io/prismatic-flipperzero/>.

The app never scores, ranks or diagnoses anyone: a detector records your marks, an assessment
records your grade.

### Assets

| File | Firmware | API | Tested on hardware |
|---|---|---|---|
| `prismatic-0.1.0-official-api88.2.fap` | official 1.5.1 | 88.2 | yes, see `docs/evidence/parity` |
| `prismatic-0.1.0-momentum-api87.1.fap` | Momentum mntm-012 | 87.1 | no (build verified only) |
| `kompendium-0.1.0.pkp` | any | | yes, the same pack bytes as the parity run |

Each FAP ships with a `.build.json` (SDK URL and SHA-256, API, source commit); the pack ships
with its build report. Everything is listed in `SHA256SUMS`, and CI re-downloads the published
files and checks them against it.

### Install

1. Download the FAP whose API matches your firmware and `kompendium-0.1.0.pkp`; verify both
   against `SHA256SUMS`.
2. Copy the FAP to `SD/apps/Tools/` and the pack to `SD/apps_data/prismatic/`.
3. Open **Apps → Tools → Prismatic**.

### What it writes

Only under `SD/apps_data/prismatic/`: quiz progress (`progress.bin`, at most 3 KiB, written
through a temporary copy) and, when you choose to save a detector's marks, `sessions.jsonl` (at
most 32 KiB). No radio transmission, no network access, no credentials.

### Limits
